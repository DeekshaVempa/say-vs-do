Who keeps their word?

Every forward-looking promise management made on an earnings call, checked against what the company reported later. The credibility score is the share of measurable promises kept, smoothed so that a company with only a few promises does not look perfect by luck. The delivery score is the same, but also counts a beat (doing better than promised) as delivered.

Guidance source: hand-labeled guidance. Transcripts and reported results are real, taken from the company's SEC filings.
